# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Challenge claims and implementations
with evidence; do not attack people. Welcome different experience levels and
give actionable, proportionate feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and
deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not post credentials, personal data,
or private agent logs in issues or discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content and restrict participation
to protect constructive collaboration. Enforcement should explain the relevant
behavior while protecting people who report concerns. Report a concern through
an available private maintainer contact; if none is listed, request one without
publishing sensitive details.

These community expectations do not alter the MIT license's permissions.
